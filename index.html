      HOW TO PLAY

      Each day you get 7 anime prompts, 25 seconds each. Every prompt has lots of right answers.

      Obvious answers charge a little. Obscure ones charge a lot. Wrong guesses cost nothing — keep trying until time runs out. Close typos get a "Did you mean…?" — press Enter again to accept.

      After prompt 7 your fighter fires. The more niche you went, the bigger and farther the blast.

      Everyone gets the same 7 prompts each day. Small typos are forgiven.
