            HOW TO PLAY

            Every prompt has lots of right answers. Obvious answers charge a little, obscure ones charge a lot. You get 25 seconds per prompt; wrong guesses cost nothing. Close typos get a "Did you mean…?" — press Enter again to accept.

            When you're done your fighter fires. Each answer summons a wave of monsters for the beam to wipe out — the rarer the answer, the bigger the wave.

            Daily Charge — 7 prompts, same for everyone, last 2 are HARD.
            Infinite — endless prompts. A skip or time-out costs one of 3 lives.
            Party — pass one device around. Everyone answers the same prompt, and answers already taken that round don't count.
